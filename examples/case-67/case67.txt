Approval Timeline: H1B extension of stay with premium processing
Posting in case this helps anyone else.

This is my first H1B extension (I filed my first petition in 2023, and was subject to the cap lottery).

My company uses Fragomen and paid for premium processing. I am not in the tech industry or in a tech role. I am not from one of the full or partially banned countries.

April 10: LCA approved

April 22: H1B petition filed

April 24: Application received by USCIS via FedEx

April 27: Premium processing clock started & receipt notice was sent. Case is assigned to the Nebraska center.

May 1: Approval notice

May 6: I-797A received in the mail by Fragomen
