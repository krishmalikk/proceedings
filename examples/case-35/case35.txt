# https://www.reddit.com/r/usvisascheduling/comments/1svvi3z/parents_b1b2_visa_approved_mumbai_india/

This is the visa experience of my parents for their B1/B2 appointment in Mumbai.

I want to first thank this sub, as it was very helpful for preparing my parents for their appointment.

Here is their visa appointment proceedings.

Once they entered the consulate and had their documents checked, they sat in the section of chair marked 'Gujarati'. They said, there were 4 language options: English, Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati.

They were then called to join the queue for the interview. They saw a lot of rejections in their line but all the 3 people/couple before them got their visa approved. They were called next.

The visa officer was a young guy with translator sitting next to him.

Visa Officer: Good Morning

Dad: Good Morning

Visa Officer: Why do you want to go to US?

Dad: To travel and visit our son.

Visa Officer: What does he do their?

Dad: He works at XYZ company

Visa Officer: I mean, what's his position?

Dad: He works as XYZ role

Visa Officer: Did he study in US and where?

Dad: Yes, he studies at XYZ university (parents were bit surprised by this question and said the full name instead of acronym, the translator told VO the acronym and location of the university for ease)

Visa Officer: What do you do?

Dad: I am a retired government officer

Visa Officer: Your visa is approved.

Honestly I had a big doubt that they will be rejected but I am so happy that visa was approved.

Hoping this helps someone preparing for their parents visa.
