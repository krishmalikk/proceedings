Tips for F1 Applicants struggling with the May, June 2026 Slot Scarcity in India
I’ve been seeing a lot of posts about the lack of F1 slots today. Having tracked the consular patterns for my clients at US Advisor, here is what’s currently happening and how you should adjust your strategy:

The "Bulk Drop" Cycle: We are currently seeing a pattern where slots are being released in concentrated bursts rather than a steady daily stream. Based on recent data, the New Delhi and Chennai consulates have been the most active for May/June slots, while Mumbai and Hyderabad are seeing significant backlogs of up to 2.5 months.

The 61% Rejection Reality: Department of State data from last month shows F1 rejection rates for Indian students reached nearly 61% in 2025. This means the Consulate is being extremely strict about "potential immigrant" intent. Your documentation needs to be airtight—generic answers about your future plans won't cut it this year.

System Time-outs: The usvisascheduling.com portal is under heavy load. If you are seeing "No Slots Available," do not refresh every 5 minutes or you risk a 72-hour IP lockout. The most success we’ve seen recently is during the "system cooling" periods between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM IST.

Third-Country Restrictions: Reminder that the option for "third-country" stamping (Mexico/Canada) ended late last year. You must secure an appointment at one of the five Indian consulates, so don't waste time looking for regional alternatives that no longer exist.
