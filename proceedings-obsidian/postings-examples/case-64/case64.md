H1B Visa Stamping Experience – Hyderabad Consulate
Hey everyone,

I wanted to share my H1B visa stamping experience at the Hyderabad consulate. Hopefully this helps someone.

Timeline
Appointment slot booked: Sunday, March 29, 4:02 AM PST

OFC / Biometrics: April 23, 9:30 AM

Consular Interview: April 30, 10:00 AM

Location: Hyderabad

CEAC status on April 30: Refused

Status changed to Approved: May 5, around 9:30 AM

Status changed to Issued: May 6, around 9:30 AM

Documents
The only documents the visa officer asked me for were:

Passport

I-797 copy

I did not carry the original I-797 because it was still with my company. I carried a printout, and it was absolutely fine in my case.

You still need to carry your DS-160 confirmation and appointment confirmation, they only checked those at the gate and nowhere else.

I was not asked for any other documents during the actual interview.

Consulate Process
There was no separate standing line specifically for H1B outside, but they were calling out H1B candidates from the line, so it moved faster than the normal line.

Total time was around 1.5 hours, including standing in line outside the consulate, security, waiting inside, and the actual interview.

The interview itself lasted probably 3–4 minutes.

Also, you do not need to wear formals. They really do not care. Wear something decent and comfortable.

Social Media Handles
In my DS-160, if I remember correctly, I had only mentioned the handles and not the full profile URLs.

During the interview, I was not asked to provide a printed copy of my social media profiles.

After the interview, I did not receive any additional emails asking for more information.

The officer did ask me to make sure all my social media profiles were public.

Interview Questions Asked
The officer asked me the following questions:

Who is your employer?

What is your job title?

What does the company do?

Do they sell products?

What are the products?

What is your salary?

Where will you be working?

How long have you been working with this company?

What did you study in the U.S.?

Why were education details not included / visible in the Visa Application?

Do you have any physical threat to stay in India?

Do you have any issues or physical threat to come back to India?

The officer asked me to place my right four fingers on the scanner.

I know people usually talk about the left hand, but apparently it does not really matter. It can be right hand, left hand, or thumbs depending on what the visa officer wants or prefers.

Result at the Interview
At the end, the officer said that my visa was currently refused and subject to further review.

She kept my passport and gave me a white 221(g) printout.

She also asked me to make sure all my social media profiles were public.

I was not asked to submit any additional documents during the interview or afterward.

Final Outcome
My status on CEAC showed Refused on April 30.

It changed to Approved on May 5 around 9:30 AM.

It changed to Issued on May 6 around 9:30 AM.

So in my case, the 221(g) / refused status resolved without any additional document submission or email.

Stay / Location Tips
For biometrics, I stayed at Avasa Hotel because it was about a 1-minute walk from the OFC location.

For the consular interview, I stayed at Casa Hotels and Suites, which was about a 5-minute walk from the consulate.

I reached the consulate about 15 minutes before my appointment, and honestly, there is absolutely no point going very early. You will still have to wait outside depending on how they are managing the line.

Takeaways
"Refused" on CEAC does not always mean final rejection. It can simply mean administrative processing or further review.

If they keep your passport, that can be a positive sign, though it is not a guarantee.

Carry all important documents, but do not be surprised if they only ask for passport and I-797.

A printout of I-797 was fine in my case.

Be prepared to clearly explain your employer, role, salary, work location, company products, and education history.

Do not over-answer. Keep responses short and direct.

There is no point reaching too early for the consular interview, You will just make a huge crowd and will have to stand under the burning sun for hours.

Formals are not necessary. Dress decently and comfortably.

If your case involves social media review, make sure your handles are accessible/public if the officer asks you to do that.

Hope this helps someone going through the H1B stamping process. Happy to answer questions based on my experience.
